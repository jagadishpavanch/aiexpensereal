### Spam and precision (CloveAI)

- **608 of 5,951 spam rows (10.2%) become a refund transaction.** Two shapes plus one stray: "Income Tax: You have pending refund of INR N. Claim now: http://tax-refundN.in" (329), "Electricity bill refund of INR N approved! Claim now: http://refund-power<city>.in" (278), and one "unclaimed refund ... Process now" text. Cause: a "refund" cue plus an amount, with no check for "pending", "approved", "claim now" or a link. This is the one failure of the precision target. Phase B's future-cue veto and a link veto fix it. These messages carry no bank sender id, so the real SMS path's sender check already blocks them; this run skips that check.
- **565 not-spam "Vi: Your recharge of INR 2GB/day for 396 is successful" rows become a ₹2 expense.** The amount reader takes "INR 2" from "INR 2GB/day" (a unit glued to the digits). A real parser bug, even though the dataset text is garbled.
- **598 "EPFO: Pension contribution of INR N received" rows become income.** Debatable: it is a contribution into the EPF account, not cash in hand. Candidate for "Needs review" rather than auto-save.
- Missed completed transactions: "Thank you for using your ICICI Bank Debit Card XX# for INR N at ..." (323) and "UPI of INR N to X from A/C XX# successful" (314) are ignored, and electricity bill reminders (BSES, UPPCL, TATA Power, about 900 rows) are ignored by the bill reader. "Income Tax Dept: Your refund of INR N has been processed. Will be credited in 3-5 days." (600) is routed to `unread` rather than ignored; it is correctly not kept.

**Design decision: people stay expenses.** Paying a person by UPI (rent, the house help) is spending, so it must not become a `transfer`; only own-account moves, card-bill payments, wallet top-ups and investments are transfers. The reader marks such payments with `toPerson` (a personal VPA or one to four plain name words with no shop or business wording, on UPI, IMPS or NEFT, never a card swipe). FinEE labels these `transfer`, so the 975 rows labelled `transfer` / `is_p2m: false` that we read as an expense or income to a person are scored correct in the category metric and listed in the scorecard as "counted correct by design". They are a deliberate disagreement with the dataset, not failures. Without that rule the category score would be 69.9%, not 90.2%.

**Behaviour changes worth knowing about**

- An amount followed by GB, MB, day, min, hr or % is never money ("INR 2GB/day"). The Vi recharge text then has no amount and is ignored (it names a plan size, not the price).
- EPFO / pension contributions are ignored (565 CloveAI rows were income before).
- A pending, unclaimed or eligible refund, and any text with a link plus "claim", "verify", "process now" and no masked account or reference, is ignored. "Refund processed, will be credited in 3-5 days" is ignored, not listed as unread.
- "Auto-debit scheduled" in an EMI reminder keeps it a bill instead of an expense; loan reminders are named "Loan EMI ··1234", not "Card bill".
- Bills read ISO due dates (`Due date: 2025-09-26`) and take the sender prefix ("BSES:") as the biller name.
